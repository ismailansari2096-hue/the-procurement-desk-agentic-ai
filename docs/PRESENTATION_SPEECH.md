### [0:30 – 1:15] Architecture (45 Seconds)
*(Stage Cue: Gesture toward the six cards displayed on the screen as you name them in sequence.)*

> **"Our architecture consists of six specialized Python agents:**
>
> 1. **Thread Understanding Agent** — extracts supplier offers, quantities, currencies, and discounts.
> 2. **Supplier Intelligence Agent** — checks supplier status and blacklist information.
> 3. **Policy Compliance Agent** — evaluates pricing limits, approval rules, and banned terms.
> 4. **Prompt Injection Defense Agent** — detects adversarial instructions, including Hindi and Devanagari patterns.
> 5. **Pricing and Negotiation Agent** — computes concession-based counter-offers and deal values.
> 6. **Decision Validation Agent** — applies decision precedence and validates the final output format.
>
> **The agents are coordinated through a deterministic Python orchestrator. This design provides repeatable decisions and traceable rule-based reasoning."**
